Abbreviations #

Short forms appear on screens where space is tight and in error messages where precision matters more than length. This is all of them.

For concepts rather than initials, see the Glossary.

Documents and operations #

Short formStands forWhat it is
ASNAdvance Shipment NoticeA supplier's notice of what they have dispatched. Not stock - a promise you can plan a dock around
AWBAir WaybillThe courier's tracking number for a shipment
BOMBill of MaterialsWhat a manufactured item is made of
GRNGoods Receipt NoteProof that ordered stock actually arrived. Posting it is what creates stock
LPNLicence Plate NumberThe id of a handling unit - a carton or pallet treated as one thing
POPurchase OrderWhat you have ordered from a supplier
POSPoint of SaleThe till screen
RFQRequest for QuotationAsking suppliers to quote before you order. Never mandatory - a purchase order does not require one
SOSales OrderA customer's order
SKUStock Keeping UnitThe unique code identifying one specific product
UOMUnit of MeasureEach, kilogram, box - how a quantity is counted

Tax and compliance #

Short formStands forWhat it is
GSTGoods and Services TaxThe sales tax, calculated automatically
GSTINGST Identification NumberA business's tax registration. Its first two digits are the state code, which is how intra-state versus inter-state is decided
HSNHarmonised System of NomenclatureThe tariff code classifying a product for tax. Required on every item, whatever its rate
GSTR-1GST Return, form 1The outward-supplies return. Nil and exempt supplies are reported HSN-wise, which is why HSN is required even at 0%
IFSCIndian Financial System CodeIdentifies a bank branch
LUTLetter of UndertakingLets exports be made without paying tax up front
PANPermanent Account NumberAn Indian tax identity
SEZSpecial Economic ZoneSupplies to one are zero-rated
TDSTax Deducted at SourceTax withheld from a payment

Money #

Short formStands forWhat it is
APAccounts PayableWhat you owe suppliers
ARAccounts ReceivableWhat customers owe you
CODCash on DeliveryPaid when the goods arrive
FXForeign ExchangeAnything involving a currency other than your own
GLGeneral LedgerThe accounting record itself
MRPMaximum Retail PriceThe printed ceiling price
P&LProfit and LossThe income statement

People, security and systems #

Short formStands forWhat it is
2FA / MFATwo-Factor / Multi-Factor AuthenticationA second sign-in check beyond the password
APIApplication Programming InterfaceHow another system talks to this one
CSVComma-Separated ValuesThe spreadsheet format used by every bulk import and export
HR / HRMHuman Resources / ManagementThe people module
JWTJSON Web TokenThe digital ID card your browser presents on each request
RBACRole-Based Access ControlPermission follows the role, not the person
SLAService Level AgreementThe time a task is expected to take. Exceeding it is an SLA breach
SSOSingle Sign-OnSigning in through an identity provider your business already uses
TOTPTime-based One-Time PasswordThe six-digit code an authenticator app shows
UATUser Acceptance TestingTrying it properly before going live

Warehouse and logistics #

Short formStands forWhat it is
ATSAvailable to SellPhysical stock minus what is reserved
DCDistribution CentreA warehouse that supplies stores rather than customers
FEFOFirst Expired, First OutPicking the earliest expiry first
FIFOFirst In, First OutPicking the oldest stock first
OMSOrder Management SystemThe screen that follows an order end to end
PIMProduct Information ManagementThe product content and readiness layer
WMSWarehouse Management SystemThe bin, pick, pack and count screens
