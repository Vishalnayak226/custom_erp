Privacy approvals #

When a security incident may need people told, someone decides Notify or Do not notify. A second person, the approver named on that decision, checks it here.

Deciding #

What it is

Privacy approvals is the list of Notify or Do not notify decisions, about a security incident, that are waiting for you as the named approver.

Why it matters

A second person checks the decision, so one person cannot decide alone. You cannot approve a decision you made yourself.

Use case

A security incident may need customers told. A colleague decided Notify and named you. You read the incident, the evidence reference and the reason, then click Approve, or Send back with a note of at least 5 characters.

How to do it

  1. Open Settings » Monitoring » Privacy approvals. The list shows only the decisions waiting for you.
  2. Read each one: the incident, who may need telling, the decision, who made it, the evidence reference and the reason.
  3. Click Approve to close it for good, or Send back with a note of at least 5 characters saying why.

You cannot approve a decision you made yourself. If the decision changed after you opened the list, the screen asks you to reload and read it again.

Who can be the approver #

An admin, or a user with the Privacy Approver role. That role reaches this one list and nothing else: no records, settings or other screens. An approver who is switched off, or loses the role, can no longer approve a decision they were named on.

To give someone the role, an admin creates it under Settings » Roles with the exact name Privacy Approver, then sets it on the user under Settings » Users. See the admin guide, A Privacy approver.
