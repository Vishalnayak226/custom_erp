Custom dashboards #

A custom dashboard is a page of widgets - numbers, bars, lines, donuts, tables and pivots - drawn from the ERP's own records. Open them under Reports → Custom dashboards.

What you see #

  • Ready-made dashboards for Sales, Stock, Buying, Customers and Money open for everyone. Copy one to change it.
  • Every figure is for the stores you may see, and money only if your role may see it. A store manager and the owner can open the same dashboard and each sees their own figures.
  • Dates, Store, Brand, Category and Channel at the top apply to every widget (a widget can keep its own dates and store).
  • A number shows its change on the period before. Point at a mark to see its value; click it to list the bills, orders or receipts behind it.
  • The page refreshes itself every few minutes while it is open.

Building one #

  1. + New dashboard (or Copy an existing one).
  2. + Add widget: a title, a dataset (sales lines, POS bills, stock on hand, stock movements, purchase orders, goods receipts, transfers, customers, loyalty points, online orders, expenses, ledger postings) or a report, a chart type, what to show it by, and what to add up. The preview draws as you choose.
  3. Arrange widgets with ◀ ▶, − + and ↕; edit with ✎, remove with ✕.
  4. Share and home: users, roles or stores; and the roles that get it as their Home screen.
  5. Save.

Taking it with you #

  • CSV / XLSX on a widget download its rows.
  • PDF saves the whole dashboard as a landscape A4 page.
  • Email me this sends it Daily, Weekly or Monthly - numbers and bars in the email, every row in a CSV - and lists it under Reports → Schedules.

If something is missing #

  • No "+ New dashboard": your role cannot build dashboards - an administrator gives Create on Dashboard Layout in Roles.
  • A dataset is not offered: your role cannot read that record type.
  • An amount says "hidden": your role does not see money figures.
  • "This widget's data cannot be shown" (REPORT-0988): edit the widget and pick its fields again.

Full steps: User Guide §9.2. Administration: Admin Guide, "Custom dashboards".
