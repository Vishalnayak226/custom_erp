Submit and settle an expense claim #

Use Expenses to move a claim from Draft through approval, verification and payment. HRM → Home counts the claims, and their amount, waiting at each step.

Submit a claim #

What it is

An expense claim records money an employee spent, with its category, amount, GST amount, advance adjusted and purpose.

Why it matters

A claim moves from Draft through approval, verification and payment, so no one is paid on a claim that nobody approved. Pending Approval is not the same as reimbursed.

Use case

An employee travels to Jaipur and pays ₹2,400 for a taxi. You create a Draft with the amount and a specific purpose, then choose Submit for Approval so the manager can decide.

A new expense claim: the spend, its category and the receipt

How to do it

  1. Select the Employee and Location. Enter Expense Date, Category, Amount, GST Amount, Advance Adjusted and a specific Purpose using the supporting receipts and approved policy.
  2. Choose Create Draft. The system supplies the claim number; retain it and check the saved amounts before submission.
  3. Choose Submit for Approval on the Draft row. Follow the approval queue for the manager's decision. Do not treat Pending Approval as reimbursed.

Verify and settle #

What it is

Finance Verify and Mark Paid are the two finance steps after the manager approves a claim.

Why it matters

Finance checks the receipt, policy, tax treatment and advance before a claim is marked paid. The button records a workflow outcome. It does not prove bank settlement.

Use case

The manager approves the ₹2,400 taxi claim. A finance operator chooses Finance Verify, checks the receipt, and then Mark Paid once payment has gone out through the approved process.

How to do it

  1. Open the manager-approved claim, check the receipts, policy, tax and advance, then choose Finance Verify.
  2. On the Verified claim, choose Mark Paid only after the approved payment is made.
  3. Check Paid against the settlement record and keep the claim and payment references.

After manager approval, an authorized finance operator chooses Finance Verify and checks the receipt evidence, policy, tax treatment and advance reconciliation. A Verified claim offers Mark Paid. Record payment only after following the approved payment process, then verify Paid against the settlement record.

The button records a workflow outcome; do not assume it independently proves bank settlement. Retain the claim and payment references for reconciliation. A rejected claim needs the reason addressed through the permitted workflow, not an unlinked duplicate claim.

If a save or transition times out, reload the claim and inspect its status before retrying. If the list is unexpectedly empty, check loading and access before entering another claim. Give support the claim number and error/correlation reference without sharing personal receipt data in ordinary chat.
