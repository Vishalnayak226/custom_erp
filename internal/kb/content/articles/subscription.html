Your Subscription #

Settings > Customize > Subscription (short code SUBS) shows a client's own plan. Only an Admin sees it. Nothing on this screen changes anything: plans are chosen and paid with the platform owner.

What the screen shows #

  • Status, in plain words: for example Paid up to 30 Nov 2026, a failed payment with the day everything keeps working until, Cancelled with the last day of access, or paused, stopped or ended. Your data is kept in every case.
  • Plan and Period ends: the paid period runs to that date. A cancelled subscription keeps working until then.
  • Grace until appears only after a failed payment. Everything keeps working for 7 days; after that the client is paused until the payment goes through, and paying resumes it.
  • This month: documents, active users and storage, each against what the plan includes. Months follow Indian time. Over shows the amount for use above the plan where the plan prices it; a meter with no price stops at the limit instead (message SAAS-0193).
  • Invoices: one GST tax invoice for each payment, with the taxable amount, CGST and SGST (within the supplier's State) or IGST (across States), and the total. An invoice still being numbered shows Pending.

When something looks wrong #

  • Only an Admin can see the subscription: sign in as the client's Admin.
  • No subscription: the client has no plan with the platform yet. Ask the platform owner.
  • A module you paid for is missing, or one you dropped is still there: ask the platform owner to reconcile the plan. No records are deleted when a module is switched off; switching it back on brings everything back.

The platform owner's side (plans, payments, reconcile, invoice settings) is in the admin guide, Packages and the SaaS subscription.
