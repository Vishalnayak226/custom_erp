The supplier portal #

A supplier signs in with its own login and sees only its own records, read-only.

Giving a supplier a login #

An administrator opens Setup » Users, adds the user with the role Supplier and links it to the vendor. That user opens straight on the supplier portal.

What the supplier sees #

What it is

The supplier portal is the screen a supplier's own login opens on: its orders, delivery notices, bills and payments, read-only.

Why it matters

Suppliers stop calling to ask what is still open, whether a bill matched or when it was paid, and they see none of your rates, MRP or internal notes.

Use case

A mill wants to know whether last month's bill was paid. It signs in, opens Bills and sees it matched and paid, with the reference under Payments.

How to do it

  1. The supplier signs in; the portal opens on their name.
  2. Orders shows their open POs and ASNs the delivery notices sent.
  3. Bills shows each bill's match and payment status, and Payments each payment.

The portal opens on the supplier's name with four tabs:

TabShows
OrdersApproved and closed POs, with the items still open
ASNsThe delivery notices sent
BillsEach bill's match and payment status, amount paid and owed
PaymentsDate, amount and reference of each payment

A supplier never sees rates, MRP, discounts, totals, charges, internal notes or approvals. Another supplier's record answers Not found.

Telling you a delivery is coming #

What it is

A delivery notice (ASN) tells your warehouse what is on its way, against which order, and when.

Why it matters

The warehouse sees it as an Expected ASN, so it can plan the dock, and the goods receipt loads its lines with Load Items from ASN.

Use case

The mill dispatches 150 of the 300 sarees ordered. It sends a notice for 150 with the carrier and tracking number, and the receiver later loads those lines from the ASN.

How to do it

  1. On Orders, click Send notice on an open order.
  2. Change the quantity if needed. It can never be more than is still open, counting earlier notices.
  3. Add the expected date, carrier and tracking.
  4. Click Send notice.

The notice reaches the warehouse as an ordinary ASN with the status Expected.
