The supplier portal #

A supplier signs in with its own login and sees only its own records, read-only.

Giving a supplier a login #

An administrator opens Setup » Users, adds the user with the role Supplier and links it to the vendor. That user opens straight on the supplier portal.

What the supplier sees #

The portal opens on the supplier's name with four tabs:

TabShows
OrdersApproved and closed POs, with the items still open
ASNsThe delivery notices sent
BillsEach bill's match and payment status, amount paid and owed
PaymentsDate, amount and reference of each payment

A supplier never sees rates, MRP, discounts, totals, charges, internal notes or approvals. Another supplier's record answers Not found.

Telling you a delivery is coming #

  1. On Orders, click Send notice on an open order.
  2. Change the quantity if needed. It can never be more than is still open, counting earlier notices.
  3. Add the expected date, carrier and tracking.
  4. Click Send notice.

The notice reaches the warehouse as an ordinary ASN with the status Expected.
