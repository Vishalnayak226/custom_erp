#### B.3.12 Finance controls — who posted, period close and reopen, bank reconciliation, control totals (Stage 73, 55.10–55.20)

Six migrations ship with this release and run with `-migrate` like any other: `migrations_stage73_1_9_gl_posting_attribution.sql`, `migrations_stage73_1_10_period_close_tasks.sql`, `migrations_stage73_2_16_bank_reconciliation_exceptions.sql`, `migrations_stage73_55_15_expense_receipt_reference.sql`, `migrations_stage73_55_15b_attachments.sql` and `migrations_stage73_55_16_asset_depreciation.sql`. All are additive; nothing existing is changed or removed. **Deploy them together with the binary** — without the new GL accounts, depreciation runs fail and disposals are refused (ASSET-0539).

**Who may post, and who may read the books (140.12).** Posting and settling money documents - sales invoices (post, settle, cancel, receipts), credit and debit notes, journals, bank reconciliation, period close and reopen, FX revaluation, landed cost, payment proposals and vendor payments - needs the **finance.post** right, held by Administrator, Admin, Accountant, Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable. Finance reports (trial balance, ledgers, statements, GST and every other Finance report) need **finance.statements**, the same right as the Finance screens, also when exported, scheduled or put on a dashboard. A till's cash difference posts to **5295 Cash Short / Over**. Card / UPI payouts from the acquirer's daily settlement file (**AcquirerSettlement**, imported like any CSV) are matched to bank credits every hour and on **Match card / UPI**. A period does not close while a till is open or awaiting approval, or while offline sales have not reached the server.

- **Who posted each GL line.** Every new GL line records **Posted by** and **Source command**: the signed-in username and the screen's API route; `service:job` and `job:<type>` for background jobs; `service:api-key:<prefix>` for a public-API key; `service:inbound` for an unauthenticated webhook; `service:background` plus the engine function for anything else. Lines posted before the release stay blank and read **before attribution** — they are not back-filled, because a guess would be worse than a blank. Users review it in Reports → Finance → **GL Posting Review** and **General Journal** (User Guide §9B).
- **Who may close.** Creating, closing and requesting a reopen of an accounting period is allowed to **Accountant** and **Super Admin** (before this release, Super Admin only). The decision this follows (D09) names "Finance Head / Admin"; there is no Finance Head role, so the Accountant role template — the one that owns period close — plays it. A tenant role literally named *Finance Head* is treated the same.
- **The close tasks are mandatory.** A period cannot close until its five tasks are signed by their owners (FIN-0501). Owners must be active users (FIN-0505); only the owner signs (FIN-0508).
- **Reopen approval rule.** The release adds an approval rule for `PeriodReopenRequest` — any value, approver role **Accountant** (Super Admin may always decide) — in **Settings → Approval Rules**. Change the role there if a different role should approve reopens. The requester may approve their own request only while **Who can approve their own documents** allows it (ADMIN_SOP §B.10; it does in an existing client as shipped - a new client starts at *Nobody*) - choose *Nobody* there to keep reopens two-person. If an approval went through but the period did not reopen (the reopen runs just after the approval is saved; a failure is logged to System Status), the period's close board shows **Reopen now** to finish it.
- **Bank reconciliation.** A new GL account **5230 Card / UPI Settlement Fees** takes the acquirer's fee when a card or UPI payout is matched (clearing 1101 / 1102 is moved to the bank account's GL code). Closing an exception as **Explained** is limited to Accountant and Super Admin. Reconciliation of statement lines dated in a closed period is frozen (FIN-0511).
- **Control totals** on Inventory Valuation, Receivables Ageing, Payables Ageing, Sales Register, GST Return Summary and Loyalty Points Liability read the documents and the GL in one consistent snapshot at the cut-off they state. A role that may not see amounts (the sensitive-column rule) sees only whether they tie. **Payables Ageing** ages the 2100 GRN Suspense sub-ledger — one open item per goods receipt, cleared by bill payments, debit / vendor credit notes and purchase-return reversals — and ties to 2100; the former PO-based view is the **Open Purchase Commitments** report.
- **Receivables vs Ledger (by invoice) (49.5.5).** Reports → Finance. When the Receivables Ageing control totals do not tie, this report says where: every posted customer invoice whose amount still owed differs from its balance on 1300 Accounts Receivable, and every 1300 posting no invoice accounts for (a journal straight to 1300, a marketplace posting, an unlinked credit note), each with what the difference usually means. Fix a difference with a credit note, a receipt or a journal, never by editing the invoice. It only reads; running it posts nothing.
- **A posted invoice is not cancelled by a save (47.16.4).** A posted Sales Invoice, Credit Note, Debit Note, Vendor Credit Note, Vendor Invoice or Journal Voucher cannot be saved back to Draft, Cancelled, Rejected, Void or Reversed (FIN-0844): reverse it with a credit or debit note, or its own reversal action, so the ledger and the GST return move with it. A Draft can still be cancelled. Since 49.5.3 the same holds for an Approved (posted) goods receipt: it is reversed with a **Purchase Return**, never saved as Cancelled (a Pending receipt still cancels). Each GST / Companies Act record control and the test that proves it is in `docs/security/gst-companies-act-controls.md`.
- **Posted ledger lines never change (47.16.4b).** The database refuses to delete a GL posting or change its account, amounts or date; correct it with a reversing entry. A backdated voucher, a month's depreciation and an offline sale get their date when they post. Two things may still write: a client's data reset (`tenantctl reset-data`) deletes the ledger, and renumbering a store, entity, cost center, department or project carries its new code onto the postings. Bank reconciliation still marks a line matched. The Super-Admin scale test (`POST /api/v1/admin/scale-test`) runs only in a sandbox client (PLATFORM-0318).
- **Part settlement.** Sales Invoice **Settle** and Vendor Invoice **Pay** take a part amount; each receipt or payment posts once under its own reference, so a retried request never double-posts. Refusals: FIN-0517 (more than outstanding), FIN-0518 (foreign currency: full only), FIN-0519 (reference reused for a different amount), FIN-0520 (invalid reference), FIN-0521 (TDS on a part-paid bill), FIN-0522 (credit note against an open foreign-currency invoice). A posted credit note naming an open invoice reduces its outstanding before any refund.
- **Scheduled reports and digests.** Reports → **Schedules** shows a Super Admin every ScheduledReport and DashboardDigest with owner, next run (the worker's own rule: Active, not deleted, next run date on or before today; checked hourly), last result and masked recipients. **Pause** sets the record Inactive; **Resume** sets it back; **Run now** (owner or Super Admin) sends through the same path as the worker and is refused while running or within a minute of the last run. All three are audited. A schedule deleted in Setup is no longer sent (before this release it kept going out). **A schedule is its creator's (Stage 73.D.9):** under Setup → Scheduled Report / Dashboard Digest each person lists, opens, edits and deletes only the ones they set up (a Super Admin all of them) — the recipients and webhook URL (which carries its token) are not shown to anyone else, and the delivery log masks the address and prints only the webhook's host.
- **Custom dashboards (Stage 100).** Reports → **Custom dashboards** (USER_GUIDE §9.2). **Who may build:** the **Create** tick on **Dashboard Layout** in Roles is "Build dashboards" — a role without it opens the dashboards shared with it and the ready-made ones but cannot make or copy one. **What each viewer sees:** a dataset is offered only to a role that may read its record type (POS bills need read on POS Cart, purchase orders on Purchase Order, the ledger on Journal Voucher...); every figure is cut to the viewer's stores (their store access, as reports are) and money (sales value, stock value, ledger amounts) is shown only to the roles that see report money columns in full (Admin, Super Admin, Store Manager) — others see *hidden*. So one dashboard shared with every store manager shows each their own store. **Sharing:** the maker shares with users, roles or stores and can make it a role's **Home** screen; only the maker or an Admin changes or deletes it. **Refresh:** Configuration → Reports → **Dashboard refresh** (minutes, default 5) is how often an open dashboard reloads; each answer is also kept 60 seconds on the server, so many people opening one dashboard run it once. **Email:** **Email me this** creates a Dashboard Digest run as its maker (their stores, their money rule); it is sent through the server's mail account (the same `SMTP_*` settings as document emails) with the charts as HTML and the rows as a CSV, and shows under Schedules like any digest. Without a mail account the digest still goes to its webhook / event and its status says no mail account is set up.
- **Report exports are private.** A background export can be listed, downloaded and cancelled only by the person who requested it (or a Super Admin). Another user asking for it by id gets "not found".
- **Expense payments.** Paying a claim now credits the bank with what is actually reimbursed (claim + GST − advance adjusted) and clears the advance from **1600 Staff Loans & Advances**, where advances are booked (Loans/Advances). Before, the bank was credited with the full claim even when an advance was adjusted.
- **Receipts on expense claims are stored on disk, not in the database.** Files live under `media_store/attachments/<tenant schema>/` in the server's working directory (`/opt/erp/media_store` on Linux), under random names, with their details in each tenant's `document_attachments` table. JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF only (checked from the file's bytes), 5 MB each, 5 per claim; changeable only while the claim is Draft or Rejected (a Super Admin can remove one later). **`deploy/backup.sh` and `manage.ps1 backup` dump only PostgreSQL**, so add `media_store/` to your backup (for example a nightly `tar` beside the dump, kept as long as the dumps) and restore it with the database; PIM product media live there too. A row whose file is missing downloads as "The stored file is missing".
- **Leave balance.** `GET /api/v1/hr/leave-balance` returns a person's own balance only, unless the role has HR self scope (Store Manager, Super Admin).
- **Fixed assets — depreciation and disposal.** New accounts `1410` Accumulated Depreciation, `5310` Depreciation Expense and `4300` Gain on Asset Disposal. Each ended month posts Dr 5310 / Cr 1410 under key `Asset:<id>:DEP:<YYYY-MM>`, dated at the month-end so period reports show it in its own month, and never twice. An `asset_depreciation` job is queued once per calendar month for every client with Fixed Assets on (checked hourly; the first check is an hour after the server starts); an Accountant or Super Admin can post at once with **Run depreciation**. **First run after this release catches up:** every Capitalised asset gets one posting per month ended since its capitalisation month (an asset capitalised two years ago gets about 24 at once); Draft and already-Disposed assets are untouched; months in a closed period are skipped until it is reopened. Disposal posts due depreciation first, then Dr 1410, Dr the cash/bank account for the proceeds, Cr 1400, and the difference to 5300 (loss) or 4300 (gain).
- **HR journeys and who sees what.** Appraisals route by the **Appraisal** approval rule (seeded: Store Manager) and grievances by the **Grievance** rule (seeded: Super Admin); whoever submitted can never decide. Grievance description and resolution notes are readable only by Super Admin and Admin, and by the employee the case is about (73.D.2 - their own case, through the record API and the Approvals inbox alike); a Store Manager lists every case without its content; other roles see only their own employee's cases — `GET /api/v1/hr/grievance-access` returns exactly this and the screens print it. The Approvals inbox now applies the same self scope and hidden fields as the record API (before, it showed every pending grievance's text to every role). Employee Self-Service cannot read AppraisalCycle or TrainingProgram, so its screens use the cycles and programs on the employee's own records. For My Requests, set the Employee's **Linked ERP User ID**.

| FIN-0503 | Changing a task on a closed period | Request a reopen |

**Posting profiles - which accounts an event posts to** (Stage 74.1.3). **Setup → Finance → Posting Profile**. Out of the box every event posts to the accounts it always has; a profile changes that for one event:

- **Event** - what is being posted: stock adjustment gain / loss (live now on **Inventory → Stock Adjustment**), and the events the coming documents use (damage, revaluation, expiry, material issue / return, store credit, job work, non-returnable challan, customer advance).
- **Only for** - legal entity, reason code, loss class, cost centre (all optional). The most specific Approved profile wins: one naming the reason code beats one naming only the legal entity. A document posts its total once, so a reason-code profile applies when every line carries that reason.
- **Debit account / Credit account** - an account code from the chart (refused otherwise); leave a side blank to keep today's account. **Effective from / to** limit it in time.
- **Maker-checker.** A profile applies only once **Approved**: submit it and an **Accountant** (or Super Admin) approves it under **Approvals** - never the person who made it. Editing an Approved profile sends it back for approval.
- **Stage 148.4 - by item group, customer group, vendor group.** **Only for** also takes an **item group** (the item's category), a **customer group** (set on the customer) and a **vendor group** (set on the vendor). The new events are Sales Invoice revenue, Sales Invoice cost of goods, POS sale revenue, POS sale cost of goods and Goods receipt (inventory side). With no profile nothing changes: 4100 / 5100 / 1200 as before. A profile moves only the revenue, cost or inventory side of that event; receivable (1300) and payable (2100) stay fixed because settlement posts to them. A sale of mixed groups splits the total by line value. A return or refund reverses through the account the original sale used; a sale made before the profile existed stays on 4100. A profile naming a missing or blocked account stops the posting with a clear message and posts nothing.
- **Stage 148.5 - Chart of accounts.** **Financial Accounting → Chart of Accounts**. **New** opens an account request: Add, Change (name, type, parent, no manual posting), Block or Unblock. A request goes to **Approvals** and an **Accountant** (or Super Admin) applies it by approving. Codes never change and there is no delete. **Parent** groups accounts: children show under the parent here, and the trial balance carries the parent. **No manual posting** (control accounts) refuses a General journal line on that account; documents still post to it. A **blocked** account refuses new postings (FIN-0870), but its history and balance stay readable and a cancellation can still undo an old document.
- Accounts added for these events: 2650 Store Credit Liability, 4400 Inventory Gain, 5320 Inventory Shrinkage, 5330 Inventory Damage Write-off, 5340 Inventory Revaluation, 5350 Expired Stock Write-off, 5420 Job-Work Conversion Cost, 5430 Job-Work Loss, 5440 Samples / Non-returnable Challan Expense. A customer advance's liability stays on its stored-value profile's account (2640), so a profile cannot split it across two accounts.


**Set-off, GSTR-1 lock and period hold (148.22, 148.23, 148.25).** All off until you act. *Finance > Lock a month after GSTR-1* (`finance.lock_after_gstr1`, off): once an Accountant marks a month's GSTR-1 filed, its sales invoices and POS sales cannot post or cancel; credit notes, receipts and returns still can. Unmarking is for full-access roles, with a reason, and is audited. A period put **On Hold** refuses the same sales postings with no setting needed. *Finance > Set-off clearing account* (`finance.setoff_clearing_account`, blank): choose an existing liability or clearing account; with it blank, set-off vouchers are refused. No account is created for you, so the release adds no accounts to any client. Set-off vouchers use the approval rule SetOffVoucher (Accountant). A sales-invoice line with an *Income account* credits that Revenue account instead of 4100.

**GSTR-2B match and exports under LUT (170.36, 170.37).** *Finance > GSTR-2B amount tolerance* (`finance.gstr2b_amount_tolerance`, 1): rupees a vendor bill's value and tax may differ from its 2B line and still count as Matched. *Finance > GSTR-2B date tolerance* (`finance.gstr2b_date_tolerance_days`, 3): days within which a bill with a different number still matches on supplier, amount and date. *Finance > Refuse an export under LUT without a valid LUT* (`finance.require_lut_for_export`, off): when on, a Sales Invoice with export type *Export under LUT* is refused (TAXGST-1720) unless the LUT register has an Active LUT covering the invoice date; off, the register is only a record. Users need the LUT register (Finance) to add a LUT; the 2B list is read-only apart from the decision. The GSTR-1 download and the 2B import need the same access as the GST Return Summary and finance posting.

#### B.3.12 RFQ to purchase order (Stages 73.2.1-73.2.3, decision D06)

- **Screen and API.** Procurement → RFQ / Quotes is its own lazy module (`public/view-rfq.js`). APIs: `POST /api/v1/rfq/save` (create, or update a Draft's lines), `POST /api/v1/rfq/{id}/invite` and `/decline`, `POST /api/v1/rfq/{id}/quote`, `GET /api/v1/rfq/{id}/comparison`, `POST /api/v1/rfq/{id}/award` (`quote_id`, optional `location`, `line_items` for free-text lines, `leave_out`). The older `/api/v1/rfq/quotes` and `/rfq/select-quote` still work.
- **Stored on the RFQ:** `lines` (line_no, sku or free text, uom, qty), `vendor_invitations` (vendor, Invited / Responded / Declined, due date, quote id; the old `invited_vendors` list is kept in step), `location`, `response_due_date`, and after the award `awarded_po_id` / `awarded_quote_id`.
- **Stored on a VendorQuote:** priced `lines` (quoted unit, conversion to the RFQ unit, unit price, tax %, lead days, not quoted), `subtotal`, `tax_total`, `freight_amount`, `landed_total` (also written to the old `quoted_price`), `payment_terms`, `valid_until`. A unit conversion comes from the quote line or the item's **UOMConversion** master. A newer quote from the same vendor marks the earlier one **Superseded**.
- **Award** runs under the RFQ's row lock: it creates a **Draft** PurchaseOrder (numbered from the PO series; `items` at the quote's price per RFQ unit, ex-GST; `freight_amount`, `payment_terms`, `quote_valid_until`, `source_rfq_id`, `source_quote_id`, `source_requisition_id`), marks the quote Selected with `po_id`, the other live quotes Rejected, and the RFQ Closed. A repeat of the same award returns the same PO; any other is refused (RFQ-0555). PO quantities must be whole units; an expired quote cannot be awarded (RFQ-0556). The PO's GST is computed from the item master as for any PO - the quoted tax % is for comparison.
- Generic edits cannot select a quote or change an awarded RFQ (RFQ-0555). Codes: RFQ-0551 (lines), RFQ-0552 (state), RFQ-0553 (vendor), RFQ-0554 (quote terms), RFQ-0555/0556 (award).

#### B.3.13 Supply proposals (Stage 73.2.18)

`SupplyProposal` (module Inventory) is written only by `engines/supply_proposals.go`: `POST /api/v1/planning/proposals/generate` (`location`, `source` reorder | mrp | backorder, `lead_time_days`, `safety_stock`), `GET /api/v1/planning/proposals?location=&status=`, `POST .../approve` (`ids`, `action` Purchase | Transfer | Production, `vendor`, `from_location`, `quantities`, `note`) and `POST .../reject` (`ids`, `reason`). One open proposal per source, item and location (regenerating refreshes it). Approval locks the proposals, converts them in one transaction into Draft documents numbered from the PO / TO / PRO series that carry `source_proposal_ids`, and records `target_doctype` / `target_id`, quantity, decider and time on each proposal; a non-open proposal is refused (PLAN-0557). The release grants HR/Admin and Store Manager read/update on SupplyProposal; approving also needs create on the target doctype. The screen is a tab of Manufacturing, so a client without the Manufacturing module uses the API. A Purchase approval for a vendor with no state is refused (PURCHA-4961, Stage 49.5.6b).

**Accepting a BOM change on a production order (Stage 49.5.6c).** When an order's BOM changed after its material was issued, the completion that closes it is refused (MFG-0276) until someone accepts the change: `POST /api/v1/manufacturing/acknowledge-bom-variance` (`order_id`, `reason`), which the Complete / Report Partial buttons call after asking for the reason. It now needs **update** on Production Order (Store Manager and the full-access roles by default; before 49.5.6c any signed-in user of the Manufacturing module could do it) and a reason (MFG-4963), and only where the BOM really changed and the order is not Completed (MFG-4964). It stores `bom_variance_acknowledged` and `bom_variance_ack` (who, when, reason, each component's quantity per unit before and after) on the order and writes a `PRODUCTION_ORDER_BOM_VARIANCE_ACK` audit row. A record-form save, an API save or a CSV import that sets, changes or clears either key is refused (MFG-4962); an import that leaves them out keeps them.

**Production costing settings (170.11, 170.13).** Under **Settings → Configuration → Manufacturing**, both default to off, and with both off production posts no ledger entries and scrap and rework only write the order's log.
- **Scrap and rework move stock and cost** (`manufacturing.scrap_rework_posting`): a scrap entry takes the stock out at cost and posts Dr 5360 Production Scrap Loss / Cr 1200 Inventory; sending to rework opens a linked Draft production order.
- **Work-in-progress accounting** (`manufacturing.wip_accounting`): material issue posts Dr 1250 Work in Progress / Cr 1200; each finished-goods receipt Dr 1200 / Cr 1250; closing the order moves any remainder to 5370 Production Variance. The choice is fixed per order when its material is issued, so switching the setting mid-order never leaves it half posted.
- **Other accounts:** a posting profile (Setup → Finance → Posting Profile) on event `production_scrap`, `production_wip_issue`, `production_wip_finish` or `production_variance` maps that posting to other accounts, optionally per legal entity. Map Work in Progress to the same account on all three WIP events, or WIP will not clear when the order closes.

#### B.3.13a Vendor price agreements and PO discounts (Stages 148.6, 148.7)

- **Where.** A `PriceListVersion` with **Vendor** set is that vendor's agreement. The same approval and effective-date rules apply (approving a new version supersedes the open one of the same code). Sales price lookups ignore vendor versions. Row columns: item, design, category, price, min/max qty, discount %, vendor code, pack multiple, count across PO, buy/get, valid from/to, commitment qty/value.
- **Refused on save.** PURCHA-4970 (quantity ranges overlap), PURCHA-4971 (gap between breaks), PURCHA-4972 (a price with no item, design or category), PURCHA-4973 (discount outside 0-100), PURCHA-4974 (buy/get/pack not a positive whole number), PURCHA-4975 (order discount out of range).
- **Every PO path prices the same way.** The PO form, the generic save, CSV import, RFQ award and supply proposals all go through the one pricing step. The order date (else the first save date) fixes which rule applies, so approving later does not re-price. With no agreement and no discount, a PO saves byte for byte as before.
- **Expiry alert.** Setting `procurement.vendor_agreement_expiry_alert_days` (default 7, 0 = off): once, that many days before a vendor agreement's end date, the event "Vendor Agreement Expiring" goes out through the notification rules (Setup → Notification Rule).
- **Volume rebates from vendors** (`procurement.vendor_rebates`, Stage 148.17, off). Off: no rebate fields, list or button is shown and every rebate route refuses (`PURCHA-5020`). On: the vendor agreement (Price List Version with a vendor) gets **Rebate on** (Gross or Net of debit notes), tier 1 and tier 2 target and percentage (tier 2 higher on both; `PURCHA-5024`); the rebate is the percentage at the highest tier reached, on the whole qualifying amount (bill value without GST, by invoice date). **Vendor Rebate Claims > Calculate** makes one claim per vendor and period; approval goes to an Accountant in **Approvals**; approval accrues Dr **1820** Vendor Rebates Receivable / Cr 5150, **Settle** raises a debit note, Dr 2100 / Cr 1820. GST is not adjusted; raise a credit note if the rebate needs it. A claim moves only through these steps.
- **Vendor link on a purchase order** (`procurement.vendor_link`, Stage 148.18, off). Off: the **Vendor link** window says "Not turned on", the public page refuses (`PURCHA-5060`) and no reminder goes. On: a buyer makes a signed link for one PO (14 days, revocable, no login, no vendor accounts); the page at `/p/vendor.html` shows only that PO's lines. A vendor's qty or date change and invoice file wait for the buyer (**Apply** re-opens the line's confirmation; an invoice file never posts). The Vendor field **Link changes apply at once** (off) lets a trusted vendor's date changes and qty cuts apply without review; a higher qty still waits for the buyer. Files are limited to PDF, PNG, JPG, 2 MB each, 5 per link. Overdue lines send a weekly reminder with the link through the outbox, only while the setting is on. The vendor's unit name is remembered per vendor (a name only, no conversion). Public requests are rate limited and audited.
- **Supplier onboarding** (`procurement.vendor_onboarding`, off). Off: vendors work as before. On: a new vendor starts **Applied** and cannot be put on a new PO until approved. Approval reuses the approvals engine (rule on `VendorOnboarding`, Store Manager) and `approvals.self_approval`; the three document ticks (GSTIN, PAN, bank proof) must be set first. A vendor with no status counts as approved, so nothing existing changes.
- **Buy only from approved vendors** (`procurement.approved_vendor_only`, off). On: a PO line whose item or category has **Setup → Approved Vendor** entries is refused for any vendor not listed. Items with no entries are unaffected.
- **Supplier logins.** A user with role **Supplier** linked to a vendor (**Setup → Users**) lands on the supplier portal (orders, ASNs, bills, payments of that vendor only) and can reach no other screen or API.
- **Vendor advances** (Stage 148.15). The advance account **1810 Vendor Advances** is added to every chart by a migration, which is why that release stops for a data-change YES. Give, apply, refund and cancel post through the normal period-lock, export-freeze and vendor payment-hold checks (a refund and a cancel are never held).
- **Bills without a PO** (`finance.expense_bills`, Stage 148.19, off). Off: no button, and the routes refuse (`FIN-0930`). On: **Vendor Invoice > Bills without a PO** enters a vendor's bill by expense account with GST per line and a cost-centre split; it books on entry (Dr expense account and any blocked GST, Dr 1500 eligible GST / Cr 2100, one journal per cost centre) and then pays, part-pays, deducts TDS, ages and holds like any bill. Over the Vendor Invoice approval amount it waits Pending Approval and books on approval. A line with no account takes the debit account of a posting rule for event `expense_bill` and the vendor group; with neither it is refused (`FIN-0932`). A plain save of a VendorInvoice cannot create or alter one (`FIN-0933`). Excluded from vendor rebates. The migration adds fields only and writes no client rows.
- **GST credit and rate by price** (`finance.itc_eligibility`, Stage 148.42, off). On: HSN rows and vendors gain **GST credit** (Eligible or Blocked) and HSN rows gain **Rate by price** (`1000:5,2500:12,:18`, lowest first, only the last open). A PO line takes its own (the **GST credit** column on **New PO**, shown only when this is on), else the HSN row's, else the vendor's, else Eligible. Blocked GST stays in the item's cost and out of input credit and the GST report; at bill match only the eligible share goes to 1500 (the blocked share is booked to stock cost by the bill-difference entry when it is Rs 1 or more). A price band sets the rate of a purchase line; no band keeps the row's rate. Wrong bands are refused (`TAXGST-1330`, `TAXGST-1331`).
- **Promise date on order lines** (`orders.promise_dates`, Stage 148.27, off). Off: orders as before. On: each new order line gets a promise date and a reason such as "2 in Mumbai, 3 on PO-123 due 3 Nov". Stock on hand ships today; what stock cannot cover waits for Confirmed PO lines and transfers in transit (Reports, Procurement, Expected arrivals). **Courier days** (`orders.promise_courier_days`, 0 to 30) count open days after dispatch. **Closed days** (`orders.promise_closed_days`) take weekdays and dates, for example `Sun, 2026-11-12`; no dispatch or courier day lands on one. **Cut-off hours** (`orders.promise_cutoff_hours`) take an hour per store or store/channel, for example `HO=15, HO/Shopify=12`; after it dispatch is the next open day. Empty means no cut-off. Nothing is reserved. A line short of stock with nothing due gets no date and "short N, none due". Reports, Sales, **Late promise lines** lists open lines past their date, by item and customer, with days late. The date is fixed when the line is made; a later change in stock does not move it. The order gets `promise_date` (the latest line date) and `promise_reason`; when a line has no date the order date is blank and the reason names the items (Stage 73.4.2.1). New Order asks `GET /api/v1/oms/promise` per line and shows a **Promised** column.
- **Risk check on new orders** (`orders.risk_hold`, Stage 73.4.2.4, off). Off: nothing runs. On: a new order is held with hold code `FRAUD_RISK` (**Risk check**) when it trips any rule: **COD order above** (`orders.risk_cod_above`, rupees), **Order above** (`orders.risk_value_above`), **Orders per phone in 24 hours** (`orders.risk_orders_per_phone`: held when the phone already has that many) or **Blocked phones and PIN codes** (`orders.risk_blocked`, comma separated). 0 or empty turns a rule off. The reasons are kept on the order as `risk_reasons`, also when another hold (payment, address) came first; releasing that hold then moves the order to `FRAUD_RISK` instead of reserving. Anyone who may release holds releases it (OMS → **Pending Orders**, **On hold** list, **Release**, or the order's **Release hold**); the order keeps `risk_cleared_at` / `risk_cleared_by` and the audit log has `ORDER_RISK_RELEASED`. No outside service is called.
- **Release backorders when stock lands** (`orders.backorder_auto_release`, Stage 73.4.2.2, off). An order no store can cover waits for stock (`ALLOCATION_FAILED`) instead of being refused, with or without allocation rules. Off: orders waiting for stock (`ALLOCATION_FAILED`) are retried by **Retry** or **Retry all waiting for stock** on OMS → **Pending Orders**. On: a worker retries them every 15 minutes, oldest first, through the same release a person runs (`POST /api/v1/oms/backorders/release` does one sweep now). Each order reserved writes the audit entry `ORDER_BACKORDER_RELEASED`.
- **Order payments** (Stage 73.4.2.3, always on). `POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/payment` with `action` `capture`, `refund`, `chargeback`, `chargeback_won` or `chargeback_lost` (plus `amount`, `reference`, `reason`); it needs **SalesOrder update**. The order keeps `payment_events`, `captured_amount`, `refunded_amount` and `chargeback_status`/`chargeback_amount`; a full refund sets `payment_status` **Refunded**, a lost chargeback **Charged back**. The same action with the same reference is recorded once. A move that does not fit the payment state is refused with `ORDERM-0470`. Each move writes an `ORDER_PAYMENT_*` audit entry. Nothing is posted to the ledger: the money goes out through the payment provider or a credit note.
- **Substitute on order lines** (Stage 73.4.2.5). An item's **Substitute item** (Item master) turns on **Substitute** for its unshipped order lines. `POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/lines/{lineId}/substitute` is a line edit (remove plus add at the same quantity and price), so the **Edit lines** limits and stock checks apply; an item with no substitute is refused with `ORDERM-0467`.
- **Carrier claims** (Stage 73.4.2.6, **OMS → Freight**). `POST /api/v1/oms/carrier-claims` (`booking_id` takes a booking id or any of its AWBs, `claim_type` Lost/Damaged/Short, `amount`, `notes`) needs **CarrierClaim create**; `POST /api/v1/oms/carrier-claims/{id}/move` with `action` `file` (`claim_ref`), `settle` (`amount` up to the claim, `settled_on`) or `reject` (`note`) needs **CarrierClaim update**. One claim may be open per booking. A claim cannot be typed, imported or edited on its list: only these routes write it (the doctype is strict). A step that does not fit is refused with `ORDERM-0471`; each writes a `CARRIER_CLAIM_*` audit entry. Nothing is posted to the ledger.
- **Freight invoice reconciliation** (Stage 73.4.2.7, **OMS → Freight**). Load the carrier's invoice as **FreightInvoiceLine** rows with the standard import (`invoice_no`, `carrier`, `awb`, `charged_amount`, optional `invoice_date`). `POST /api/v1/oms/freight/reconcile` (**FreightInvoiceLine update**) takes every line still Unmatched or No booking, finds the newest booking with that AWB (`provider_awb`, `awb_number` or `tracking_number`) and sets `expected_amount` (the courier's quote, else the booking's `shipping_charge`), `difference` and the status: **Matched** within **Freight invoice tolerance** (`orders.freight_tolerance`, rupees, default 1), **Overcharged**, **Undercharged**, **Duplicate** (the AWB is already on a decided line; `duplicate_of` names it) or **No booking**. One run at a time per tenant; decided lines are never re-checked. Each run writes a `FREIGHT_INVOICE_RECONCILED` audit entry. Store Manager and Super Admin have both doctypes.
- **Book a return at the store that sold it** (`returns.centralised`, Stage 77.8, off). Off: a bill from another store is returned into the store that takes it, as before. On: the return (`SR` number, credit note, reports) belongs to the store that sold the bill (`return_location`); the taking store is kept as `collected_at`, lists the return on **Returns**, does approve, receive, QC and refund there, and pays cash from its own till. After QC the goods are booked back into the selling store by disposition, then one **TransferOrder** per bucket (selling store → taking store, `source_return` set, numbered from the selling store) is dispatched and received at once: Good into the taking store's **Inward**, Damage into Damage, QC hold into QC hold. A bucket the selling store does not use is skipped and named in `transfer_note`. A transfer that cannot finish stays Approved and the return's `transfer_state` reads `Open: <reason>`; finish it on **Transfers**. Rerunning QC never makes a second transfer. Exchanges are refused on such a return. The return list is matched on both `return_location` and `collected_at`.
- **Hold a piece for a customer** (`inventory.piece_holds`, Stage 148.41, off). Off: no hold can be placed. On: **Serial numbers** shows **Hold piece**. A hold names a customer and may carry an expiry day and an advance (taken to the customer's account like a till advance). The till, invoices, dispatch and picking refuse the piece for anyone else; the named customer's sale uses it and ends the hold. A day-end job frees an expired hold; the advance stays on the customer's account for you to refund or use. A held piece cannot be moved by a transfer. Switching the setting off stops new holds but holds already placed still block; use **Release**. Reports, Inventory, **Held pieces** lists them. A hold does not touch the voucher reservation on the piece.
- **Default vendor.** The Item field **Default vendor** decides which vendor's PO a purchase proposal joins.
- **Free units** count in ordered quantity (over-receipt checks, open-PO supply) and are received at zero price; the receipt carries a blended cost, and the vendor bill matches the discounted line value.

#### B.3.13a-1 Purchase rate history and the vendor scorecard (Stages 148.12, 148.13)

- **The bill sets the purchase rate.** A **Matched** vendor bill sets each item's **Standard Cost (ex-GST)**, the purchase rate, to the rate on the bill (the bill's own lines when it has them, else the rate on the receipt, ex GST). The receipt's price is provisional until then. This is the one change an existing client sees on release day: the rate moves on every matched bill. A bill that is on hold sets nothing; releasing it (match again) does. A foreign-currency bill sets nothing. The same happens on all three paths: **Match** on a bill, the step-flow receipt's bill, and a bill made by **Upload from Excel**. **MRP never changes** by this.
- **History.** Each change is kept: who, when, old rate, new rate, the bill, the vendor and any reason. It shows on the item's edit form under **Purchase rate history** (loaded when opened). A bill matched again changes nothing and adds no row.
- **Rate jump check** (`procurement.price_jump_percent`, %, default 0 = never asks). Above it, a bill that moves an item's rate up or down by more than that percent is held with the message to enter a **Reason for the rate change** on the bill and match again; the reason is kept in the history.
- **Scorecard** (**Vendor** edit form under **Scorecard**, the RFQ comparison's **Track record** row, and the report **Vendor scorecard**, R9040). Nothing is stored; each number is worked out when asked, for the orders placed in the period (default last 12 months; the form offers 3, 6, 12 and 24). A measure with nothing to measure shows **No data**, never 0%.
  - **On time** = receipts on or before the order's expected delivery date, of the receipts whose order has a date.
  - **Fill rate** = quantity received (never more than ordered, per item) of quantity ordered, over orders that have a receipt or are past their expected date.
  - **QC reject** = (quantity received minus accepted) of quantity received.
  - **Price variance** = billed rate against the ordered rate, weighted by accepted quantity; plus means dearer than ordered. The billed rate is the bill's lines, else the receipt's invoice rate.
  - **Bill mismatches** = bills now on hold for a mismatch, and their share of the bills against those orders (a bill released after a fix no longer counts).

#### B.3.13b Charge codes (Stage 148.8)

- **Where.** Setup → Procurement → **Trade Charge Code**, **Trade Charge Rule** and **Trade Charge Entry** (read only). `Freight terms` is on the Vendor. Super Admin has full access; HR/Admin and Accountant can read, create and update; Store Manager can read. The doctypes are `Trade*` because `ChargeCode` is the warehouse labour billing code (Stage 42.6).

A step GRN's typed invoice total is taken to include vendor-paid charges. **Used for** Selling is stored for later: selling documents do not read charge codes yet. Weight basis is not limited to jewellery. A booked bill cannot be un-booked from here.

#### B.3.14 Quality and maintenance (Stage 55.13)

Screens: **Manufacturing → Quality Inspections / Certificates / Non-conformance / Maintenance Orders** (lazy module `view-quality.js`; the endpoints are gated by the *quality* module, the tabs sit in Manufacturing). Set up before first use:

- **Inspection plans** (Setup → Inspection Plan): the item, sample size, **Applies to** (Incoming / Production / Outgoing) and the parameters with their specification. An inspection on a plan must record a Pass/Fail reading for every parameter.
- **A hold code of category Quality** (Setup → Hold Code) — used when a failed incoming or outgoing inspection holds stock. The first active one is the default.
- **Reason codes of category Quality** (Setup → Reason Code) — the root causes a non-conformance investigation picks from.
- **Maintenance schedules** (Maintenance → + New Schedule) with a checklist; the background scheduler raises a Scheduled order on each due date and copies the checklist onto it.

Rules the server enforces (`engines/quality_inspection.go`, codes QUALIT-0561..0566): an inspection names a source that exists (Incoming = a GRN and an item received on it; Production = a production order; Outgoing = a sales order) and, for Incoming/Outgoing, a location and quantity; the result is derived (one Fail fails it). A production inspection is decided only through Approvals (the order's QC gate). An incoming/outgoing one is decided once: Pass → Accept; Fail → **Hold stock** / **Rework** (a Hold for the inspected quantity, in the same transaction — released only by an approved hold release) or **Use as-is (concession)** (a note, and not by the inspector). Every failed inspection raises one NonConformanceReport linked to it. A report needs a Quality root cause, then a corrective action with an **owner** (and optional due date), then closing **evidence** (`closed_by` recorded). Maintenance orders go Draft → Scheduled (a date) → In progress (`started_by`) → Completed (notes, every checklist task ticked, parts with quantities, hours, `completed_by`/`completed_at`); parts are recorded, not issued from stock. Each action needs the role's create/update permission on the doctype (HR/Admin and Store Manager have them by default). A Certificate of Analysis records your own lot test results and is not a regulatory certificate; rejecting one moves the lot's binned stock to QC-Hold.

**Assets: leases, CWIP, adjustments, meters, maintenance cost (ask 170.5-170.9).** Setting `assets.maintenance_costing` (default off): when on, completing a maintenance order issues its parts from stock and posts the cost (Dr 5314, Cr 1200 and 5315). New accounts, added by migration: 1420 right-of-use asset, 2450 lease liability, 4310 rental income, 5311 ROU depreciation, 5312 lease interest, 1450 CWIP, 3500 revaluation reserve, 5313 impairment, 5314 maintenance expense, 5315 labour absorbed. An hourly worker posts due lease months (idempotent per lease and month); **Post due** on the lease list runs it by hand. Roles: Super Admin and HR/Admin create leases; Store Manager reads. Adjustments and CWIP actions are recorded in the audit log.

#### B.3.17 E-invoice (IRN) — GSTIN set-up, the queue, cancellation and the outage path (Stage 75.2)

A GSTIN with turnover above ₹5 crore must report every B2B invoice and credit note to an Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) and print the IRN and QR it returns. This is built so that switching it on for a client is set-up, not a build. **Today only the test provider exists** (it behaves like an IRP: IRN, Ack, signed QR, rejections, time-outs); the IRIS IRP adapter is written once the IRIS sandbox is available (75.2.11), and production needs IRIS's approval and your CA's go-ahead (75.2.13).

**Set up a GSTIN** — Financial Accounting → **E-Invoice Settings** (admins) → **+ New GSTIN**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **GSTIN** | The seller GSTIN, checked with its check digit. It is matched to documents through the store's Legal Entity (Setup → Location → Legal Entity → GSTIN). |
| **Provider** | *Test provider (sandbox only)* until the IRIS adapter exists. |
| **Turnover** | ₹5-10 crore, or ₹10 crore and above — the second also applies the **30-day rule**: an invoice older than 30 days cannot be reported, so the ERP warns at 7, 25 and 28 days and then stops it for a person (Manual review). |
| **Effective from** | Documents dated before it are never sent. |
| **Supplies to another GSTIN of the same PAN** | Whether those transfers are e-invoiced (ask your CA). |
| **Document / supply types** | What this GSTIN e-invoices (INV, CRN; B2B, SEZ, exports). |
| **E-invoicing is on** | Off by default. On: posted invoices and credit notes of this GSTIN are picked up within a minute. |
| **Send automatically** | Off by default: an accountant presses **Submit** on the workbench. On: each valid document is sent as soon as it is picked up. |
| **Whitelisted server IP** | The production IRP accepts calls only from whitelisted Indian IPs — record here the address you gave the IRP (the droplet's, `curl -4 https://icanhazip.com`). Never rebuild the droplet without changing the IRP's whitelist first. |
| **Credentials** | Stored encrypted (the connectors' key) and never shown again; leave blank to keep them. The test provider needs none. |

Every save raises the rules' **version** (shown as *v2 from 2026-10-18*); every decision records the version it was made under. **Test connection** checks the provider answers and the credentials sign in.

**Turnover warning** (Stage 47.16.4d). Switching e-invoicing on stays your decision; the ERP does not do it for you. Setup readiness warns when a GSTIN's posted sales invoices, before tax, pass ₹5 crore in a financial year (April-March) while its e-invoicing is off or not set up: the *Tax* step's check *E-invoicing is on for every GSTIN with sales over ₹5 crore in a financial year* names the GSTIN, the amount and the year. Switch the GSTIN on here, or confirm with your CA why not. It is a warning: it never holds the setup. It counts invoices of every store whose legal entity carries that GSTIN, B2B and B2C; credit notes are not deducted, so it warns early rather than late. The readiness checks come with the setup path (`GET /api/v1/setup/path`) and show under the *Tax* step of **Guided setup** (Stage 47.16.4h.2).

**Till sales count toward the turnover (Stage 47.16.4h.1).** The ₹5 crore check adds the till's paid and settled bills to the invoices: each bill counts before tax (a bill with no GST split, at its net amount), in the financial year of the day it is dated - an offline sale on the day the till rang it up - at the GSTIN of its store's legal entity. Returns are not deducted. Other GSTINs under the same PAN are not added in yet.

**Which server talks to which IRP.** `IRP_ENVIRONMENT=production` on the server makes it the production environment; anything else is the sandbox. The environment is part of every record's key, so a sandbox document can never be retried in production.

**What is never sent:** a sale to a buyer with no GSTIN (B2C — the till's bills too), a supply inside the same GSTIN, advances and store credit, types and supplies the GSTIN's rules leave out. Each is still recorded as *Not required* with the reason.

**How a document moves** (the IRN state is separate from the invoice's own status): *Ready* (frozen at posting: the e-invoice JSON and its SHA-256, checked — HSN of 6+ digits, GST unit, PIN, state, GSTINs, a number of at most 16 characters, totals in paise) → *Queued* → *Sending* → **IRN generated**, or *Rejected* (fix the masters, then **Validate** rebuilds it), or **Unknown** (the IRP did not answer: the ERP looks the document up before anything is sent again — never shown as success or failure). Failures retry with growing waits; after 8 they go to *Manual review*. A switched-off GSTIN or a refused server IP goes to *Blocked - set-up*.

**After the IRN:** the invoice's tax details (number, customer, store, lines, amounts) cannot change (`EINV-0983`). To correct it: **Cancel IRN** within 24 hours (tax role, a reason code, approved by another Accountant — Approvals; the number is never used again, `EINV-0981`), or a credit note after that. Cancelling after 24 hours moves the record to *Manual review*.

**The IRP's evidence is frozen (47.16.4f).** Once a record has an IRN, the database refuses any change to its IRN, Ack No, Ack Date, signed invoice, signed QR and the JSON that was sent, and refuses to delete the record. Each call that returned an IRN also keeps the IRP's response as received (`irp_attempts.response_body`); a session token, key or password in it is masked before it is stored.

**Outage path:** an IRN made on the IRP's portal is entered on the record (**Enter portal IRN**) and counts only after a second person verifies it (USER_SOP §11.1).

**Alerts** (the ops alert webhook): `IRP_UNKNOWN_AGING` (Unknown over 2 min), `IRP_QUEUE_AGING` (queued over 5 min), `IRP_AUTH_FAILING` (5 failed sign-ins in a row), `IRP_IP_BLOCKED`, `IRP_30DAY_RISK_7/25/28`, `IRP_CANCEL_WINDOW` (under 2 h left on an asked cancel), and a daily `IRP_RECONCILIATION` when a GSTIN has documents without an IRN. **Reports** (Finance): *E-Invoice / IRN Status Register*, *E-Invoice Error / Retry Report*, *IRN Portal Manual Upload Report*.

**E-way bills** (Stage 130.16, 74.4) - `engines/ewaybill.go`, table `ewb_documents` (kept on a client reset, like the IRN tables). **Configuration → Inventory**: **E-way bill needed from** (`gst.ewb_threshold`, ₹50,000), **HSN codes that need no e-way bill** (`gst.ewb_exempt_hsn`, comma-separated, prefixes allowed; HSN 99... services never need one), **E-way bill provider** (`gst.ewb_provider`: none - the portal plus *Record from portal* - or the test provider for training). The decision is stored with its rule version (EWB-R138-2026.2 since 47.16.4e: a sales invoice's value includes its GST - each line read as the e-invoice reads it, tax added to a line that stores only its taxable value); the bill's own status (Ready, Submitted, Generated, Failed, Expired, Cancelled, Reconciled, NotRequired) is kept apart from the document's. A live provider adapter waits for credentials (20.30 / 20.31), as the IRN one does. Errors: **TAXGST-1320**.

**Where it lives:** `engines/einvoice.go` (rules, canonical document, validation), `einvoice_provider.go` (the provider boundary and the test provider), `einvoice_worker.go` (queue and worker, every minute), `einvoice_controls.go` (locks, cancel, manual import, alerts, reconciliation, reports); tables `irp_profiles`, `irp_documents`, `irp_attempts`, `irp_events` (append-only) — `db/migrations/migrations_stage75_2_einvoice.sql`.

#### B.3.18 OMS steps, the two bills, and the Inventory documents (Stage 88)

**OMS settings.** **Setup → OMS → OMS settings** is OMS's configuration page: channel connectors, unmapped channel SKUs, bundles and every OMS list (USER_GUIDE §9A.13). The working pages are in the **OMS** menu: Orders, Pending Orders, Pick, Pack, Ship, Returns, Settlements. Old bookmarks to *Fulfillment* open Pick, and to *Marketplace* open Ship.

**Channel listings and the product sheet (146.4.5, 146.5.4).** Pull, link and the per-listing **Stock sync** tick are for administrators; the stock push (channel SKU mapping and Shopify links) leaves out an item whose listing is switched off, and behaves as before for items with no pulled listing. Only Shopify can list; the other connectors are refused with INTEGR-4800. The Item list's **Product sheet** needs both create and update rights on Item, ProductAttributeValue and ProductContent, takes a file up to 16 MiB, shares the bulk-upload rate limit and row cap of the other imports, and is CSV only.

**The Pending gate.** Marketplace and website orders wait on **Pending Orders** until someone accepts or rejects them, and the answer is sent to the channel through the job runner (retried with backoff; one that fails every retry stays on the order as *Failed* with a **Retry**). Orders keyed in by staff skip it. To let channel orders through on their own, turn on **Settings → Configuration → OMS → Accept channel orders automatically**. Rejecting cancels the order, so it needs a **Cancellation** reason code; a short-pick needs a **Short Pick** one - the release seeds the usual ones (CAN-*, SP-*). The channel must be set up (**Setup → PIM → Channel**, with its credentials) for the answer to go out; Amazon and Flipkart take accept / reject in their own seller portals. Pick and pack also work for a client with OMS but no WMS.

**The two bills (decisions D4, D5).** Pack makes the **OMS bill**, one per package, from the series **OB** (*OB/<store>/2627/00001*, 16 characters at a two-letter store code - Settings → Customize → Prefix Configs). Its number is the GST invoice number. Handing the manifest over makes the **ERP bill** (a Sales Invoice carrying the OMS bill's number) and posts it; only that posts to the ledger. If a post is refused (a closed period, say), the Sales Invoice stays Draft for Finance to post and the error is on **Settings → Monitoring → Settings → Monitoring → System Errors** (source *oms-erp-bill*). Packages billed before this change keep their Sales Invoice. The OMS bill prints from its own template (**OMS Bill**, Setup → Printing → Templates → OMS); a printer chosen for *Sales Invoice* prints it too. The order's shipping charge and its GST rate are copied onto the first OMS bill and from it to the ERP bill, which posts the same figures as the printed bill (Stage 148.46).

**Stock adjustments (D13).** **Inventory → Stock Adjustment** posts at once unless there is an approval rule for **InventoryAdjustment** (**Settings → Approval Rules**; the amount is the value moved, either way) - then it waits for someone other than its maker. It posts at the moving-average cost against **5190 Stock Adjustment** (added by this release): Dr 5190 / Cr 1200 for stock taken off, the reverse for stock added. Each line needs an **Inventory Adjustment** reason code. Numbers come from the **ADJ** series. Store Managers may raise adjustments; give another role **create** on *InventoryAdjustment* to let it.

**MRP changes (D14).** **Inventory → MRP Change** changes items' MRP from an effective date (series **MRP**, no store). A later date is applied by the job runner at midnight of that day; until then the change can be cancelled. Only HR/Admin may make MRP changes as shipped (Store Managers read them); grant **create** on *MRPChange* to change that.

#### B.3.19 Setup Workbook — a whole organisation's setup from one Excel file (Stage 128)

**Settings → Configuration → Setup Workbook** (Admin and Super Admin). Everything the [PO setup guide](po-setup-guide.md) asks for, in one Excel file instead of twenty screens. It is also the way to correct a setup later, and the way to start the next client from the last one.

- **Matching.** A row that finds a record updates it with the cells that are filled; a row that finds nothing creates one. **A blank cell leaves a value as it is**; to empty a value, type `(none)`.

- **One at a time.** Only one workbook is applied to an organisation at once. A run cut off by a server restart shows **Interrupted**; upload the same file again.

#### B.3.19a Go-live: scope, structure, finance start, cutover and the gate (Stages 47.12 and 49.17.3)

**Settings → Configuration → Go-live** (Super Admin only) has five pages. They are a record and a sign-off: nothing else in the ERP reads them, so they hold no sale, receipt or posting back, and they are empty until someone uses them.

1. **Go-live scope.** Answer the ten questions (industry, countries, legal entities, tax registrations, channels, locations, roles, inventory tracking, accounting basis, devices) and press **Save**. Every save is a new version with who and when. Each choice shows **Supported**, **Preview** or **Unsupported**, read from the capability register; a choice no register entry covers is Unsupported, never recommended. Saving turns nothing on.
2. **Structure.** The legal entity, its tax registration, its locations, warehouses and cost centres, read from your masters and drawn as boxes: green is fine, red has a problem, dashed yellow is missing, grey is out of scope. Click a box to see what is wrong, an example, an **Open** button to the record, and **Out of scope** (with a reason) or **Bring back**. Nothing is deleted or disabled.
3. **Finance start.** Calendar (a period open today), currency (INR active), chart (the posting accounts), invoice series, a bank account on the ledger, opening balances, whether stock, receivables and payables tie to the ledger, and the opening reconciliation **accepted by a named person**. Acceptance is refused while the books do not tie.
   *Cutover migration line (Stage 47.12.7):* *Migration control totals tie to what was loaded* shows, for each staged import that stated totals, the stated and loaded amount, pass or fail and the difference. An import not loaded yet keeps the line open; rejected or expired ones are left out; with no totals stated anywhere the line is out of scope. Device proof stays something a person attaches.
4. **Cutover.** Open exceptions, the backup and restore drill the host recorded, the role and separation-of-duties review, connector and device proof, and the opening reconciliation.
5. **Go-live gate.** Supported capability level, hardened deployment, credential owner and rotation, external destinations, recovery time and point targets, incident contacts, data and retention basis, and customer acceptance.

Every check shows **Pass**, **Fail**, **Not evidenced**, **Waived** or **Not applicable**. A check the ERP can read (periods, chart, control totals, roles, credentials, the host's backup log) is read live. One only a person or the host can prove is **Not evidenced** until someone presses **Evidence** and records a note and a reference; it is never Pass on a guess. **Waive** needs a reason and an expiry within a year and shows who and when; **End waiver** brings the check back. **Sign off** records your name and is refused while a Critical or Blocking check is Fail or Not evidenced. **Evidence on the connector checks goes stale:** *Connectors proven in use* and *External destinations confirmed* record the destinations (active credentials and webhooks) they were attached for; when one is added or switched off, the check is **Not evidenced** again and says so. Attach it again. If the credential list cannot be read, the check says it could not be read instead of reading as no credentials. The person who waived a Critical check cannot be the one who signs. Anyone who waived a Critical check, or attached the evidence that makes one pass, cannot sign that pack; another person signs. A signature stays on the page: if a check opens again later, the line next to it reads *Reopened*.

#### B.3.20 Shopify — products, prices and stock out; orders, payments and refunds in (Stage 99)

**Who owns what.** The ERP owns products, prices and stock; Shopify owns online orders and their payments. A price or stock figure changed in Shopify admin is overwritten at the next send (the sync log says so). Shopify POS is not used — the ERP's own POS bills the stores.

**1. In Shopify** (admin of the client's store): Settings → **Apps and sales channels** → **Develop apps** → **Create an app**. Under **Configuration → Admin API access scopes** tick: `read_products`, `write_products`, `read_inventory`, `write_inventory`, `read_locations`, `read_orders`, `write_orders`, `read_customers`, `write_merchant_managed_fulfillment_orders`, and `read_shopify_payments_payouts` if the store uses Shopify Payments. **Install** the app, then copy the **Admin API access token** (shown once) and, under **API credentials**, the **API secret key**.

**2. In the ERP:** Setup ▸ PIM ▸ **Channel** → **+ New** (Platform **Shopify**, Channel Type **Website**, Status **Active**) → save. Its row now has a **Shopify** button, which opens the channel's Shopify page:

| Section | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1. Connect** | Shop address (`yourstore.myshopify.com`), the admin API access token, and the API secret key as the **webhook secret** → **Save**. A box left blank keeps what was saved, so you need not retype the token to change the secret. **Test connection** shows the shop's name and locations and **names any access the app is missing** — add those scopes in Shopify, reinstall, and test again. |
| **2. Stores, prices and what syncs** | Map each Shopify location to the ERP store whose stock it sells (an online order ships from the store mapped to the location Shopify chose). **Safety buffer**: units kept back from every quantity sent (never below 0). **Price list Shopify sells at**: a price list code, or blank for each item's sale price; an automatic offer (no coupon) lowers it, and the MRP goes as Shopify's compare-at (struck-through) price. Switch **Send prices**, **Send stock** and **Take orders in** on or off. **Save**. |
| **3. Webhooks** | **Register** (later **Register again**) points Shopify's order, refund, fulfilment, checkout and app-removed notices at the address shown — this client's own address. Every notice is checked against the webhook secret; without it every notice is refused. A notice Shopify sends twice (same delivery id) is handled once; one that failed is handled again on Shopify's retry. **Changing the secret:** save the new one here first - the old one is still accepted for 7 days - then change it in Shopify. The same applies to BigCommerce's webhook secret. |
| **4. Products, prices and stock** | **Publish designs not on Shopify yet** sends up to 50 per click (it says how many are left): each design is one product with its sizes / colours as options and variants (SKU, barcode, price, MRP as compare-at, weight), HSN as a metafield, the brand as the vendor, and its pictures (each variant's own picture where it has one) on the first publish. **Publish it** sends one design or item by code. The metafield definitions are created in the shop on the first publish that needs them (the app needs `write_products`; if the shop refuses, the metafields are still sent and a warning is logged). A changed set of pictures is sent again: the old ones are removed first. A product published before this release only records its pictures and sends none until they change. Needs no new scope. The same setting sends the product's typed relations as the shop's product-recommendation metafields (`shopify--discovery--product_recommendation`: `related_products` and `complementary_products`, type list of products; Similar / Alternative / Upsell are related, Complete the look / Cross-sell / Add-on complementary; at most 25 each, in Order order). Only related items already published to that shop (they have a Shopify product) are listed. A product with no relations sends no such metafield, and removing every relation later does not clear what the shop already holds.


**What runs by itself** (every channel's **Sync interval**, 15 minutes by default): orders updated in Shopify since the last pull (the backstop for a missed webhook); prices that changed; stock that changed, per location, sent in one grouped call — and all stock once a day; payouts once a day. **Recent runs** at the foot of the page lists every run with its count and error.

**Orders in.** Each Shopify order comes in once (its Shopify id), whole: its number (#1001), each line's price after its discount, shipping charge, order discount, the tax lines split into CGST / SGST / IGST, payment gateway and status, COD, email, billing and shipping address and the state for place of supply. The customer is found by mobile number or added (a contact erased under the privacy rules is never re-added). A paid or COD order is released to the OMS as any channel order is; an unpaid one waits on its payment. **An edit or cancellation made in Shopify** is applied to the order until picking starts; after that it waits in OMS → **Channel changes** for a person (make the change, or return it once shipped, then **Done**).

**Back to Shopify.** When a task is dispatched (handover or direct dispatch) Shopify gets the fulfilment with its lines, carrier and tracking number, so the customer gets Shopify's shipping email — part shipments too. Cancelling an order in the ERP cancels it in Shopify (stock back, no automatic refund); marking a shipment **RTO** tags the Shopify order. A refund made in Shopify after the order was invoiced raises a **Customer Return** against the invoice through the returns path; before that it waits in **Channel changes**. Each send is a background job; on a server with outside sending off (a test server) it is recorded as *Simulated*.

**Payouts.** Each Shopify Payments payout comes in as one settlement line per order (Shopify's fee as the commission), batch `SHOPIFY-<payout>`, and the marketplace reconciliation posts and matches them (fees to 5200); the payout then matches its bank line in **Bank Reconciliation** like any marketplace payout.

**If something is wrong:** *The Shopify connection is not ready for this* (`CONN-0994`) — save the shop address and token, test the connection, map a location. *Invalid webhook signature* in the log — the webhook secret is not the app's API secret key. Orders not arriving — **Register again**, then **Pull orders now**. A live check on a development store: [`../operations/connector-live-verification.md`](../operations/connector-live-verification.md).

#### B.3.22 Bin rules, the bin generator and store shipping limits (Stage 146.3 and 146.4)

**One item per bin.** On a **Zone**, set **Bin mix rule** to *Mixed* (default), *Single item* or *Single item single lot*. A **Bin** can carry its own rule, which beats its zone's. Putaway, Location Movement, Bin Replenishment and lot putaway all refuse a second item (or a second lot) into a bin that has stock, and the message names both. Stock already in a bin is never moved by changing the rule.

**Home bins.** Add rows under **Home Bin** (Inventory): SKU, Bin code, Priority (1 first) and **Allow other items**. Putaway's **Suggest Bin** offers the first usable home bin and says so ("home bin X (priority 1)"). A bin that is Full, Blocked, in a count, over capacity or refused by its mix rule is skipped, and the reason is shown. If no home bin is usable the normal putaway strategy runs. A home bin with **Allow other items** = No takes only its own SKU. Load many at once with **Import** on the Home Bin list (columns `sku, bin_code, priority, allow_other_items`).

**Bin generator.** **WMS > Storage & Control > Bin Generator**: pick the Location (and Zone, optional), fill **Aisles** `A-C`, **Racks** `01-12`, **Shelves** `1-4`, **Bins** `1-6` and an optional **Code format** (default `{aisle}-{rack}-{shelf}-{bin}`). **Preview** lists the codes and how many are new; **Create** makes only the new ones, so running it again, or with wider ranges, never duplicates. Shelf and position are saved on each Bin (**Level**, **Position**). **Print labels** prints a label per bin; with no ranges it prints the bins already at the Location (and Zone, when one is picked).

**Bin labels.** The **Bin** list has **Print labels** too: it prints the ticked bins, or every bin on the page when none is ticked. Both buttons open one dialog: pick the **Template** (the built-in *Bin label 50 × 25*, or any sticker template whose design uses the bin fields), set **Copies** (1-20) and press **Print**. A preview opens; **Print** there sends the labels to this computer's sticker printer (§B.3.4), with the browser print as the fallback. **A4 sheet** keeps the old Code 128 sheet in a print window. The dialog remembers the last template on this computer. To design your own, open **Setup → Printing → Templates → Stickers and barcodes**, start from *Bin label 50 × 25* and use the **Bin** fields (bin code, store / warehouse, zone, aisle, rack, shelf, position; the barcode carries the bin code). Bin designs are not offered for item stickers.

**Pick sequence.** Set **Pick sequence** on a Bin to put it earlier or later on pick lists (lower first). Bins without one follow in the usual zone, aisle, rack, bin order.

**Store shipping limits.** On each **Location**: **Ship channels** (comma list; blank = all), **Online orders** (No keeps it out of online routing), **Daily order cap** (0 = none), **Ship priority** (1 first). **Settings > OMS** holds, for each of channel, online switch, cap and distance, whether the rule is *hard* (store left out) or *soft* (store ranks lower; the default is hard), and **Farthest store to ship from** in km (0 = off). Import PIN, latitude and longitude into **PIN Code** (your own file; none is supplied). *Nearest Pincode* then uses real distance when the order's PIN and every candidate store's PIN are in the master, and the old PIN-number difference otherwise. A store with none of these set, and a tenant with no PIN master, route as before. A fixed channel store (a channel tied to one location) and return routing are not limited. The cap counts orders sent to the store since the business day began.

#### B.3.23 Credit control on the order (Stage 148.3)

**Set the limit and the rule.** On a **Customer**, **Credit limit** is the ceiling. A customer with no limit is never held. **Stop invoices over the limit** (*Yes*) refuses the invoice at posting (`FIN-0861`); anything else only warns, and the posted invoice carries the warning. Customers that already had a limit were marked *Yes* by the upgrade, so nothing they could do before is now allowed. **One-time customer** (*Yes*) keeps a walk-in buyer out of exposure and history. **Payment terms** fill the order's and invoice's terms and due date.

**What is counted.** Exposure = the customer's approved, unpaid invoices + their other open orders + this order. It is checked when an order is accepted. Over the limit, the order goes On Hold with the reason **Over credit limit** (`FIN-0860`), shows who owns it and since when, and a **Credit Hold Release** request goes to the **Store Manager** in the approval inbox. Approving releases the order and sends it on to Pick; rejecting leaves it held. A Credit hold cannot be released or re-held by hand (`ORDERM-0980`).

**The customer link.** Orders and invoices now carry a customer link beside the typed name. The upgrade filled it only where the typed name matched exactly one customer; a name that matched none or several stays unlinked, and the limit is then looked up by name as before.

**FX revaluation and Intercompany entry** on the Journal Vouchers screen call the same routes as before and need the Finance posting permission.

#### B.3.25 Bank files, part matching and GL source lines (Stage 170)

- **Statement import** (`POST /api/v1/finance/bank-statement/import`, screen Bank Reconciliation / Bank files): CSV (bank_account optional, date, description, reference, amount, Debit/Credit) or MT940. Each line gets a stable id from account, date, amount, direction, reference and running balance, so a reloaded file adds nothing. Needs `finance.post`.
- **Bank response** (`POST /api/v1/finance/payment-proposal/{id}/bank-response`): CSV with payment_reference, utr, status, reason. Paid records the UTR; Rejected files a bank exception owned by the loader. No payment is reversed automatically.
- **Part match**: parts are kept in `bank_match_parts`; the auto and rule matchers leave an entry that has parts alone. Error codes FIN-1700 to FIN-1704, FIN-1706 and FIN-1707.
- **GL source line** (setting `finance.gl_source_line`, default off): when on, new postings of a sales invoice carry `source_line_id` per document line (each account spread over the lines in proportion). Off, postings are unchanged. Other posters leave it empty.
- **Receivable / payable by group (148.4b)** is not built yet; accounts stay 1300 / 2100.

#### B.3.23a Brand-restricted dealers (Stage 148.43)

Off by default: **Settings → Sales → Brand-restricted dealers** (`sales.brand_restrictions`). Off, orders ignore brands and the customer form does not show the boxes. On, a **Customer** shows **Allowed brands** (Brand names or codes, comma separated), **Brand rule** (*None*, *Warn* or *Block*) and optional **Rule from** / **Rule to** dates. A customer with no list, or a rule of *None*, is unrestricted; outside the dates the rule is idle. An order line whose item brand is not on the list is refused with `SALESR-1400` (*Block*) or placed with a **Brand note** on the order (*Warn*). The check runs when an order is created and when a line is added to an open order (a warning is not repeated on an added line). Items with no brand are never refused.

#### B.3.25 Expense limits, advances, project costing and the service clock (Ask 170.29-170.34)

Every item below is off or inert until you set it up. Nothing changes for existing data.

- **Expense limits.** **Setup -> Expense Category** holds each category with a **Limit per claim** and a **Limit by grade** table. The claimant's grade comes from the new **Grade** field on the employee. A grade limit beats the plain limit. **Settings -> expense.limit_action**: `approval` (default) saves an over-limit claim and sends it to the role in **expense.over_limit_approver_role** (default Super Admin); `refuse` blocks the save with EXP-1700.
- **Employee advances.** **HRM -> Staff Advance**: **Pay advance** posts Dr 1600 Staff Loans & Advances, Cr 1100 once (EXP-1702 on a second try). The report **Employee advance balance** shows advanced, settled and open per employee. **expense.net_employee_advance** (default off): when on, **Mark Paid** on a claim clears the employee's open advances, oldest first, from what the claim owes, and pays only the rest. Admin, HR and Accountant see every advance; anyone else sees only their own.
- **Timesheets.** Set **Cost rate (per hour)** on the employee (payroll-restricted field). Approved timesheets post Dr 5450 Project Labour Cost (on the project) and Cr 5460 Project Labour Absorbed. Map other accounts with the posting event `project_labour_cost`. A timesheet for an employee with no rate cannot be sent for approval (FIN-1721). Approval is by Store Manager. A Store Manager and an Accountant see every timesheet; a Cashier sees only their own.
- **Milestones and budget.** The project gets **Contract value**, **Estimated cost** and **Revenue method**. **Complete milestone** on a billable milestone raises one Draft sales invoice for the project, once. The report **Project budget vs actual** compares the budget lines with the ledger.
- **Project revenue.** **projects.revenue_recognition** (default off). The monthly run, also available as **Run revenue** for a month, books unbilled revenue (1330) or deferred revenue (2600) against 4100 for projects with a revenue method, once per project and month. Events: `project_revenue_asset`, `project_revenue_deferral`.
- **Service SLA.** **service.sla_enabled** (default off). Hours by priority are in **service.sla_response_hours** and **service.sla_resolution_hours** (like `Low=24,Medium=8,High=4,Critical=1`), counted in service desk hours. A late open ticket is escalated once to **service.sla_escalation_to** (`user:<id>` reassigns it, `role:<name>` flags it), the event `service.ticket_escalated` is published and the audit log notes it. Bad text is refused with FIN-1725.

### B.4 Where to Look When Something Seems Wrong

1. **`.\manage.ps1 logs`** — the fastest first check. Shows the server's own output and error logs, plus the database log.
2. **Activity Log** (in the app sidebar, formerly "Log Hub") — shows audit trails and recorded system errors from inside the running application.
3. **`docs/operations/incident-runbook.md`** — the full incident-response procedure: severity levels, escalation, rollback, and exactly where every kind of log lives. Read this before an incident happens, not during one.
