# What this system is

One shared notebook for the whole business. Sales, stock, purchases, staff and
money all go into the same place, so the cashier, the warehouse, the accountant
and the owner are looking at the same figures at the same moment.

That is the entire idea. Everything else is detail.

## Why that matters in practice

The version of this business you are used to probably keeps sales in one place,
stock in another and money in a third, and spends real effort every week
reconciling them. Here there is nothing to reconcile, because there is only one
copy. When a cashier completes a sale, stock falls and the ledger moves in the
same instant, without anyone telling them to.

The consequence you will feel first is that **you do less typing and get more
refusals**. A screen that already knows the answer will not ask you for it, and
a screen that would have to guess will stop you instead. Both are the system
working.

## Five ideas that make every screen readable

**Records.** Almost everything is a record of some type - an Item, a Vendor, a
Location, a Sales Order. Every screen is either a list of records or one record
open. Once you can see that, an unfamiliar screen is just a familiar shape with
different columns.

**Masters versus transactions.** A *master* is a thing that exists on its own and
changes rarely: a product, a supplier, a shop, a bin. A *transaction* is
something that happened at a point in time and refers to masters: a sale, a
receipt, a transfer. You set up masters once; transactions accumulate forever.
Most "why can't I do this?" moments are a missing master.

**Documents have a state.** A purchase order is a Draft, then Pending Approval,
then Approved. Nothing skips a state, and what you are allowed to do depends on
which state a document is in. An Approve button that is not there is usually a
document that is not yet in a state that can be approved.

**Your role decides what you see.** The sidebar lists only the modules your
business has licensed and your role can open. Within a screen, buttons you
cannot use are not shown - so a missing **New** button is a permission, not a
bug. See [Your first sign-in](first-login.md).

**Everything is logged.** Who did what, when, and to which record. This is not
surveillance so much as the reason it is safe to give people real access: a
mistake can always be traced and understood rather than argued about.

## What it will not do for you

It will not invent data you have not entered. An empty system genuinely can do
nothing, which is why the first hour is spent creating a location, a supplier
and a product rather than selling anything.

It will not let two people quietly disagree. Where a decision needs a second
pair of eyes - approving spend, changing a price, writing off stock - it is
routed to someone else on purpose, and the same person cannot both raise and
approve.
