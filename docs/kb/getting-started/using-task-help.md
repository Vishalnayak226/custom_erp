# Find help for your task

Choose **Help for this screen** in the application header. A short dialog opens
over the screen with the part of the handbook for that screen: what it is and
how to do it. Press **Esc** or choose **Close** to go back to where you were.
Screen coverage is still being completed; a general topic is not evidence that
every operation on that screen is documented.

## The full lesson

Choose **Open the full Knowledge Center** in that dialog. The Knowledge Center
opens on the same handbook, at the section for your screen, with that section
outlined. In the sidebar, the handbook sits under its main module (the same
names as the app's menu) with that module expanded, and the handbook lists its
own sections. Choose a section there to jump to it.

A screen's lesson has up to four parts:

| Part | Tells you |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | The transaction in one or two lines |
| **Why it matters** | What it protects or saves you from |
| **Use case** | A worked example from a store |
| **How to do it** | The steps, in order |

Screen pictures load as you scroll to them. Use **Knowledge Center** in the
navigation to browse every topic yourself.

## Search or browse

Type at least two characters in **Search help...**. Use the task name, a record
name or the error code shown by the application. Choose a result to read the
topic, or use the grouped topic links in the sidebar. The help search searches
instructions, not your business records.

Follow topic headings in reading order and check the prerequisites before
performing an action. The [role journeys](role-journeys-overview.md) provide a
starting point for a new role. For a failed action, start with
[Reading an error code](error-codes.md).

You can navigate links and the search box with the keyboard. If a control cannot
be reached or its meaning is unclear with assistive technology, report the task,
browser/device and the control that blocked you.

## If the instructions do not match

Stop before an irreversible business action. Give support the screen/task,
article title, application version, and error/correlation ID if one is shown.
Describe what you expected and what appeared. Do not send passwords, session
tokens or unrelated customer records. A help article does not grant permission
or override your organization's approved procedure.
